04.2
The Rules
Six · No Exceptions
1
Treat the card like debit.
If the cash isn’t in checking right now, it doesn’t go on the card. The limit is not your budget.
2
Autopay = statement balance.
Both cards. Not the minimum. This makes missing a payment or carrying a balance nearly impossible.
3
Keep each card under 10%.
~$100 on the Cap One, ~$30 on the OpenSky. Lower is better. Pay before the statement closes to report a tiny number.
4
Cap One is the daily driver.
Route already-planned spending through it for the 1.5%. Cash back on something you didn’t need is a 98.5% loss.
5
Leave the OpenSky nearly dormant.
One tiny recurring charge, autopaid. It exists only to age and add a second line of history.
6
Never say “I’ll pay it next month.”
Statement balance, every month, no exceptions. That sentence is how the debt starts.
